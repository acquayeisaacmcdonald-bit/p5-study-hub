JHS Maths Formulas

All the key formulas for JHS 1, JHS 2, and JHS 3 — in one place.

🧮 Algebra

Expansion
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
Expansion
(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
Difference of squares
a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)
Quadratic formula
x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a
Change of subject
If y = mx + c, then x = (y − c) / m
Slope of a line
m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
Equation of a line
y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)

📐 Geometry

Area of rectangle
A = l × w
Area of triangle
A = ½ × b × h
Area of circle
A = πr²
Circumference of circle
C = 2πr
Area of trapezium
A = ½ × (a + b) × h
Volume of cube
V = l³
Volume of cuboid
V = l × w × h
Volume of cylinder
V = πr²h
Pythagoras' theorem
a² + b² = c²
Sum of angles in triangle
180°
Sum of angles in quadrilateral
360°

📏 Trigonometry

Sine ratio
sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
Cosine ratio
cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
Tangent ratio
tan θ = opposite / adjacent
SOH CAH TOA
sin = O/H, cos = A/H, tan = O/A

📊 Statistics

Mean
Mean = sum of values / number of values
Median
Middle value when data is ordered
Mode
Most frequent value
Range
Range = highest − lowest

💯 Percentages & Ratios

Percentage
% = (part / whole) × 100
Percentage change
= ((new − old) / old) × 100
Simple interest
I = (P × R × T) / 100
Compound interest
A = P(1 + r)ⁿ

🚀 Speed, Distance, Time

Speed
Speed = Distance / Time
Distance
Distance = Speed × Time
Time
Time = Distance / Speed

🔢 Number Bases

Binary to decimal
1010₂ = 1×2³ + 0×2² + 1×2¹ + 0×2⁰ = 10
Decimal to binary
Divide by 2 repeatedly, read remainders bottom-up
