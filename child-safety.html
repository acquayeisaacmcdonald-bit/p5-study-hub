Child Safety

Learn how to keep yourself safe at home, at school, online, and everywhere you go. Read each lesson, take the quiz, and remember — you are never alone.

🚨 In an Emergency — Call These Numbers

📞 All Emergencies 112 👮 Police 191 🚒 Fire Service 192 🚑 Ambulance 193 💚 ChildLine Ghana 1800 11 222

These numbers are free. You can call them from any phone, even without airtime.

📚 Safety Lessons

Remember: If something feels wrong, it probably is. Tell a trusted adult — a parent, a teacher, an aunt, an uncle, or any grown-up who makes you feel safe. You will never be in trouble for telling the truth.

You matter. You are loved. You deserve to be safe.
